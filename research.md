Research article: A study of the effects of goal orientation on the reflective ability of electronic portfolio users

My personal goals (Family, Financial, Professional, Recreational) are mastery-oriented; about who I am and want to become. while the course's academic goals are more performance-oriented (about demonstrating technical competence, mastering HTML/CSS/JS, building a portfolio to show employers) One of the course's stated learning outcomes is to create and maintain an electronic portfolio of scholarly and design work, and my four categories are exactly the personal content that fills that portfolio. — and Cheng & Chau (2013) show that the strongest ePortfolios combine both, which is exactly what I'm trying to do.

Cheng, G., & Chau, J. (2013). A study of the effects of goal orientation on the reflective ability of electronic portfolio users. The Internet and Higher Education, 16, 51–56.
